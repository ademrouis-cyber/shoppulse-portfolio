Business interpretation

From numbers to next steps.

Key findings

  • T-Shirts have the highest total revenue at €280.
  • T-Shirts also have the highest gross profit at €168.
  • Hoodies generate €270 revenue but only 6 units were sold in the sample.

Suggested next steps

  • Track performance over a longer period before making major decisions.
  • Review product costs and profit margins regularly.
  • Investigate whether marketing or product changes could improve Hoodie sales.

These findings describe only the fictional sample. They do not establish future demand or prove that a particular business action will work.
